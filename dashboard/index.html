How the implied value is modelled

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier × breadth multiplier

Category multiple

A token is a claim on the cash its category generates. Perpetuals exchanges earn fees on every trade, so they carry 0.38× TVL; bridges earn a thin spread on parked capital, so they carry 0.10–0.14×. The multiple is a property of the category, never of a name, and the full table is in the data.

Growth multiplier

1 + clamped 7-day TVL change × 0.6. A protocol up 20% in a week scores 1.12×; one down 30% scores 0.82×. The change is clamped to −50% … +150% so one wild week cannot dominate the board.

Maturity multiplier

0.55 + up to 0.45 by age, capped at 3 years. A protocol that has run for years with no token has survived more cycles and built a larger base to distribute a token to than one listed last month.

Breadth multiplier

1 + 0.02 per chain beyond the first, up to 10 chains. A protocol live on many chains reaches more wallets and more liquidity than a single-chain one at the same TVL.

Every row’s four multipliers are shown in its detail view, so any number on this board can be re-derived from the snapshot alone. Protocols whose token already trades are kept apart below: their market cap is the market’s own value, never a modelled number.
