Token Radar

Every protocol that records no token yet, ranked by what its token would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is — and what to do to farm it before it launches.

An implied value is this radar’s own claim, from the snapshot’s figures alone. It is not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when. A protocol on this board may still have a token its DefiLlama record does not carry.

Model radar-3: multiples fitted from the snapshot’s 101 live-token comparables, plus a fitted size curve (TVL / $50M)−0.48. Walk-forward: 12.2% lower error than the flat-multiple model going forward, 8.8% reversed. See “How the implied value is modelled” below for the full table.

How the implied value is modelled

implied value = TVL × category multiple × size multiplier × growth multiplier × maturity multiplier × breadth multiplier

Category multiple — fitted, not guessed

Each category’s multiple is fitted from the snapshot’s own live-token protocols: the median market-cap-to-TVL of that category’s live tokens, divided by the size factor so a category is not credited for merely being small, then shrunk toward the prior table with weight n/(n+4) and capped at a 2× move. Lending fits 0.16× (its live tokens trade at 0.13× TVL); liquid staking fits 0.14× (0.04×); bridges fit 0.20–0.28× (0.99–1.13×). Categories with fewer than 3 live comparables keep the table value. The full fitted table is below.

Size multiplier

A token’s market cap does not grow one-for-one with TVL. Across the snapshot’s 128 live tokens, log market cap rises only 0.52 per 1.0 of log TVL: small protocols trade near 0.87× TVL, mid ones near 0.31×, large ones near 0.08×. The factor is the fitted power law (TVL / $50M)−0.48, clamped to 0.25–4: a $5M protocol scores ~3×, a $500M one ~0.33×, a $5B one 0.25× on size alone.

Growth multiplier

1 + clamped 7-day TVL change × 0.6. A protocol up 20% in a week scores 1.12×; one down 30% scores 0.82×. The change is clamped to −50% … +150% so one wild week cannot dominate the board.

Maturity multiplier

0.55 + up to 0.45 by age, capped at 3 years. A protocol that has run for years with no token has survived more cycles and built a larger base to distribute a token to than one listed last month.

Breadth multiplier

1 + 0.02 per chain beyond the first, up to 10 chains. A protocol live on many chains reaches more wallets and more liquidity than a single-chain one at the same TVL.

Every row’s five multipliers are shown in its detail view, so any number on this board can be re-derived from the snapshot alone. Protocols whose token already trades are kept apart below: their market cap is the market’s own value, never a modelled number.

Walk-forward check — tested, not asserted

The model was fitted on the 44 live-token protocols listed before 2024 and tested on the 51 listed in 2024 or later, then reversed. Mean absolute log error predicting market cap from TVL: radar-3 1.634 vs radar-2 1.862 (12.2% lower; wins 33 of 51). Reversed: 1.415 vs 1.552 (8.8% lower; wins 22 of 44). The size curve alone accounts for most of the gain (forward 1.643); the fitted multiples add the rest. Neither split was used to pick the constants.
